Create a complete, production-ready UI/UX design for a modern Uzbek marketplace platform.

IMPORTANT:
This is NOT just a simple online store. The platform has a business model where sellers/business owners pay the platform for access to sell their products for a specific period through monthly, yearly, or other subscription plans.

The platform has 3 main roles:

1. SUPER_ADMIN
2. SELLER
3. USER / CUSTOMER

For Phase 1, design ONLY the WEB version.
Do NOT design the mobile application yet.
However, create the web architecture and components in a way that can later be adapted to mobile.

The goal is to create a highly attractive, trustworthy, modern marketplace platform for the Uzbekistan market that can compete visually and functionally with major international marketplaces while being adapted to Uzbek users and local business needs.

==================================================

1. GENERAL PRODUCT CONCEPT
   ==================================================

Create a multi-vendor marketplace where:

* Business owners can register as sellers.
* Sellers can create and manage their own products.
* Sellers can upload product images, descriptions, prices, discounts and inventory.
* Sellers must choose a subscription/package to sell on the platform.
* Sellers can make monthly/yearly payments.
* Super Admin controls the entire ecosystem.
* Customers can browse products without registration.
* Customers can search, filter and sort products.
* Customers can register and create a personal account.
* Customers can add products to favorites.
* Customers can add products to cart.
* Customers can place orders.
* Customers can contact sellers.
* Customers can view seller profiles.
* Customers can leave ratings/reviews.
* The platform earns revenue from seller subscriptions and potentially additional platform services.

The design should feel:

* Modern
* Premium
* Trustworthy
* Fast
* Simple
* Business-oriented
* Friendly
* Localized for Uzbekistan
* Scalable for thousands of sellers and products

Avoid copying the exact UI of existing marketplaces.
Create an original visual identity.
